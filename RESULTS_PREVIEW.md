# ICRA results preview

These values are numerical sanity checks from the current submission specification. They are **not empirical deployment estimates**. The final paper figures are regenerated by `src/icra_evaluation.py`.

## 1. Deadline-aware feasibility frontier

For a 95% deadline-completion target, the maximum offered load (A^*) is:

| normalized deadline (d) | (M=1) | (M=2) | (M=4) | (M=8) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3.1 | 0.034 | 0.429 | 1.821 | 5.201 |
| 4.0 | 0.251 | 1.071 | 2.941 | 6.841 |
| 6.0 | 0.501 | 1.454 | 3.422 | 7.398 |

Ordinary stability would only require (A<M). The gap can therefore be substantial.

## 2. Oversight Paradox / detector-capacity co-design

Synthetic binormal ROC stress test:

- (d'=3);

- (pi=10^{-3});
- (d=4).

System-level EHC-maximizing operating points:

| operators (M) | optimal TPR | optimal FPR | offered alert load (A) | max EHC |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 0.910 | 0.048 | 0.986 | 0.872 |

Thus the same detector should be operated more selectively when human capacity is lower.

## 4. Equal mean utilization, different arrival structure

With

$$
A=1.2,quad M=3,quad ho=0.4,quad d=4,
$$

and mean-one Gamma/exponential service:

| arrival process | timely completion (Q) |
|---|---:|
| Poisson | 0.978 |
| batch size 2 | 0.971 |
| batch size 4 | 0.934 |
| batch size 8 | 0.802 |
| two-state MMPP | 0.956 |

So a system at only 40% mean utilization can fall below an EHC-style 95% deadline target solely because of temporal correlation.

## 5. Non-exponential-service robustness

For the same (A=1.2,M=3,d=4), batch-size-8 timely completion remains much lower than independent Poisson arrivals across:

- deterministic service;
- Gamma (CV=0.5);
- exponential service;
- Gamma (CV=1.5);
- lognormal (CV=1).

Representative Poisson versus batch-8 values are approximately:

| service distribution | Poisson | batch 8 |
|---|---:|---:|
| deterministic | 1.000 | 0.820 |
| Gamma (CV=0.5) | 1.000 | 0.812 |
| exponential | 0.979 | 0.788 |
| Gamma (CV=1.5) | 0.943 | 0.782 |
| lognormal (CV=1) | 0.979 | 0.798 |

The burst effect is therefore not specific to the M/M/M service assumption.

## 6. Baseline counterexamples

Current stress-test examples include:

- (ho=0.25), TPR 0.99, but short (d=2): EHC about 0.855;
- Perkins high-RST fan-out gives (PFO=6.13); (N=6) is stable under that boundary, yet a finite deadline (d=4) gives only about 0.084 completion probability near saturation;
- recall-maximizing a synthetic detector can create alert load well above human capacity despite TPR essentially 1;
- at (ho=0.4), batch-8 demand gives EHC/timely completion around 0.80.

These examples are not claims that the baseline metrics are defective. They demonstrate that stability, fan-out, recall, and average utilization do not by themselves provide a deadline-constrained end-to-end guarantee.
